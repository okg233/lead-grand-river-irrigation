What We Do

Irrigation Services

"We offer a wide variety of services. From irrigation to landscape lighting, we not only maintain your landscape, we will help you show it off."

🛠️

Irrigation Design & Installation

Full irrigation system design and installation for residential, commercial, municipal, and industrial properties. Custom-designed to your landscape and water needs.

🌸

Spring Start-Ups

Complete spring activation service for your irrigation system after winter. We inspect, test, and adjust your system to make sure everything is running perfectly for the season.

❄️

Fall Winterizations & Blow Outs

Proper winterization and blow-out of your irrigation system to prevent freeze damage. Essential to protecting your investment every fall season.

📈

Regular Maintenance

Scheduled maintenance programs to keep your irrigation system running at peak performance all season long. We handle adjustments, head replacements, and program updates.

⚡

System Upgrades

Upgrade your existing irrigation system with modern controllers, smart technology, and water-efficient components. We work with all makes and models.

💧

Water Efficient Upgrades

Reduce water usage and save money with water-efficient upgrades — smart controllers, weather sensors, high-efficiency heads, and drip zones.

🌿

Drip Irrigation

Precise drip irrigation for gardens, flower beds, shrubs, and trees. Delivers water directly to the root zone — more efficient and better for plant health.

🏭

Fertigation

Fertigation systems deliver liquid fertilizer directly through your irrigation lines for efficient, even distribution across your lawn and garden.

💡

Low Voltage Landscape Lighting

Enhance and showcase your landscape after dark with low voltage LED landscape lighting design and installation. Highlights paths, trees, garden beds, and more.

📍

Private Utility Locating

Before any dig, we locate private underground utilities on your property to ensure safe, damage-free installation of irrigation lines.

🧻

Open Face Trenching

Open face trenching to depths up to 26" for irrigation pipe installation and underground utility work on residential and commercial properties.

🔥

Vibratory Ploughing

Vibratory ploughing for cable, telephone, and satellite wire installation — minimal surface disruption, fast and precise.
